Testing Instructions for Order Form:

1. Start the server:
   bin/dev
   (or: rails server)

2. Navigate to: http://localhost:3000/orders/new

3. Test "Add Product" button:
   - Click "Add Product" button
   - You should see a new row appear with:
     * Product dropdown
     * Quantity field (default: 1)
     * Unit Price field (readonly, empty initially)
     * Remove button

4. Test product selection:
   - Select a product from the dropdown
   - The Unit Price field should automatically fill with the product's price
   - The Total should update

5. Test adding multiple products:
   - Click "Add Product" again
   - Select a different product
   - Both items should show in the list
   - Total should be the sum of (quantity × unit_price) for all items

6. Test quantity changes:
   - Change the quantity of any product
   - The Total should recalculate automatically

7. Test Remove button:
   - Click "Remove" on any product row
   - That row should disappear
   - Total should recalculate

8. Test form submission:
   - Fill in customer information
   - Add at least one product
   - Click "Create Order"
   - You should be redirected to the order show page

Fixed Issues:
- Product dropdown now properly populates when clicking "Add Product"
- Unit price updates correctly when selecting a product
- Total calculation works for dynamically added items
- JavaScript properly handles all events
